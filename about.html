About PaintCalc

PaintCalc is a small collection of free, focused calculators built for one job: helping people plan a painting or wallpapering project without guessing at the numbers.

Our method

Every calculation here is based on published industry rules of thumb: standard door and window deductions, typical paint coverage rates (350–400 sq ft/gallon), and standard wallpaper roll dimensions. Sources and assumptions are listed under each tool's "How It Works" section.

These are planning estimates, not professional quotes. Always confirm coverage numbers against your specific paint or wallpaper product's label before purchasing.

Why this site exists

Most paint calculators online are attached to big-box retailers and only account for the products they sell. PaintCalc is retailer-neutral — the math works the same whether you're buying from a hardware store, an online retailer, or a local supplier.
