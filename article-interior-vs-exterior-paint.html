Interior vs exterior paint: what's actually different

They look almost identical on the shelf, but the two formulas are built to survive completely different conditions — swapping them causes real problems.

Flexibility

Exterior paint is formulated to expand and contract with temperature swings without cracking. Interior surfaces don't experience anywhere near that temperature range, so interior paint is formulated to stay rigid instead, which improves scrub resistance.

UV resistance

Exterior paint contains UV-blocking additives to prevent fading and chalking from constant sun exposure. Interior paint skips these additives, which keeps costs down and often improves color accuracy indoors.

Mildew and moisture resistance

Exterior formulas include mildewcides and are built to shed water. Interior paint isn't designed for direct water exposure — that's why bathrooms and kitchens use interior paint at a higher sheen (satin/semi-gloss) rather than switching to exterior paint.

VOC levels and off-gassing

Exterior paint often has higher VOC (volatile organic compound) content since it's used outdoors where ventilation isn't a concern. Using exterior paint indoors can cause noticeably stronger and longer-lasting fumes in an enclosed space.

What actually happens

If you use the wrong one

Exterior paint indoors: Stays softer/tackier longer, off-gasses more, and rarely reaches the same washable hardness as true interior paint.

Interior paint outdoors: Cracks and peels within one to two seasons since it can't handle temperature swings, rain, or UV exposure.

Mixing on trim that's exposed both ways (like a covered porch): use exterior-rated paint — it's built to handle worse conditions than interior paint, so it's the safer choice when in doubt.

Common questions

FAQ

Is there such a thing as paint for both indoor and outdoor use? +

Yes, some product lines are labeled "interior/exterior" and formulated as a middle ground — durable enough outside, low-VOC enough for indoor comfort. They typically cost more than single-purpose paint.

Does the calculator work the same for exterior paint? +

Yes — the area math is identical. Just check your specific exterior paint can for its coverage rate, since textured siding or brick can reduce coverage to 200-250 sq ft/gallon.
