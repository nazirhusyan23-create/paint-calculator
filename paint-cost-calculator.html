Common questions

FAQ

How much do professional painters charge? +

Most US interior painters charge $1.50–$3.50 per square foot of wall area, or $2–$6 per square foot of floor area for a full room including labor and basic materials. Rates vary heavily by region.

Do I need primer? +

Primer is worth adding when painting new drywall, covering a much darker color, switching from oil-based to latex paint, or painting a stained or repaired wall. Skip it for a simple color refresh over a similar, well-sealed shade.
