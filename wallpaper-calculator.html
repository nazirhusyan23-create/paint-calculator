How this calculator works

01

Wall area

Same formula as paint: 2 × (length + width) × height, minus doors (21 sq ft each) and windows (15 sq ft each).

02

Coverage per roll

Roll width (converted to feet) × roll length, minus your chosen waste factor for pattern matching.

03

Round up to whole rolls

Wallpaper can only be bought in full rolls, so the result always rounds up — never buy a fraction of a roll.

Common questions

FAQ

How much waste should I add for a pattern repeat? +

Add 15-20% waste for wallpaper with a pattern repeat, since matching the pattern across strips wastes material. Plain or textured wallpaper without a repeat needs only about 10% waste allowance.

What is a standard wallpaper roll size? +

A standard single roll is about 20.5 inches wide and 33 feet long, covering roughly 56 square feet before waste. European rolls are often narrower but longer — always check the label on your specific roll.
